Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

SimoBen respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the SimoBen application and its connected services.

Information we handle

When you connect an account through OAuth, SimoBen may receive account identifiers, authorization information, and access credentials required to provide the requested integration. We only request permissions needed for the enabled feature.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, provide requested publishing or integration features, maintain the security of the service, and respond to support requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with connected service providers only as necessary to provide a feature you explicitly enable or when required by law.

Security

OAuth credentials are protected using server-side security controls and are not displayed by the application's public status endpoints.

Data choices

You can stop using an integration by disconnecting it or revoking the application's access from the relevant provider. You may contact us to ask questions about privacy or data handling.
